    Not legal advice. This is a student research prototype trained on judgments from the ILSI dataset (LeSICiN, Paul, Goyal & Ghosh, AAAI 2022). Consult a qualified advocate for any real matter.

    IPC → BNS correspondences: checked against the correspondence table compiled at CAPT Bhopal (secondary source, not the official MHA table). Mappings that could not be verified are withheld. Models: fine-tuned InLegalBERT (law-ai) and a TF-IDF + logistic-regression baseline.
